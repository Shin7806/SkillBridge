# Code of Conduct

SkillBridge is about people teaching each other, and this project works the same way. We want it to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so. Report problems through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or by contacting a maintainer directly.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
